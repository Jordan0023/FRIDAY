NETGEAR

RAX29 and RAX30 · Firmware V1.0.14.108

A single unauthenticated LAN request to the genuine UPnP service reliably terminates the daemon with a segmentation fault. Fault attribution identifies a missing optional-port parser branch and a null dereference, distinct from the publicly documented callback-overflow class.

Exposure: confirmed on the LAN-side UPnP listener. Stock WAN reachability and whole-router denial of service are not established.

What earns a place here

  1. Reachability: the attack path is available without valid administrator credentials.
  2. Attribution: the attacker-controlled input is tied to the fault or execution sink.
  3. Reproduction: the security effect repeats with a benign differential control.
  4. Novelty: a dated public-record review finds no matching disclosure or fix.
  5. Caveat: public novelty cannot exclude a private, embargoed, or reserved duplicate.
